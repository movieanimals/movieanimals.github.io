About Toth Movie Animals

Founded in 1998, Toth Movie Animals has been the industry's leading provider of trained animals for entertainment productions. Our expert trainers work with a diverse range of species to deliver reliable, safe, and impressive animal performances.

We prioritize animal welfare and ethical treatment above all else, ensuring our animal actors are treated with the utmost care and respect. Every production is approached with meticulous planning and professional execution.
