About Toth Movie Animals

Based near Los Angeles, Toth Movie Animals is a trusted partner for filmmakers seeking to bring their creative visions to life through animals. With a deep-rooted passion for wildlife and storytelling, we strive to create unforgettable moments while prioritizing the safety and well-being of every animal involved.

Founded by Derek Toth—a third-generation animal trainer—our team carries forward a legacy of dedication and care. While Derek has had the privilege of working on high-profile projects, he remains grounded in his commitment to environmental stewardship and animal advocacy. Whether it’s training animals for film or ensuring their comfort on set, Derek approaches every task with humility and respect.

When not on set, Derek enjoys hiking, exploring nature, and advocating for animal rights. These passions inspire our mission at Toth Movie Animals: to enrich stories through animals while fostering a culture of kindness and responsibility.

Our Services

Animal Training for Film & Television

We specialize in providing expertly trained animals for commercials, TV shows, and feature films. Our trainers ensure that each animal performs safely and effectively under the most demanding conditions.

Private Pet Training

In addition to our work in entertainment, we offer personalized private training sessions for pets. Our goal is to help owners unlock their pets’ full potential in a nurturing environment.

Animal Safety Consulting

Collaborating with the Animal Protection Agency (APA), we provide comprehensive oversight to ensure the well-being of animals during film shoots, live events, and press junkets. Safety is always our top priority.

Location Scouting & Wildlife Abatement

Our team assists productions in finding ideal filming locations while mitigating risks posed by local wildlife. From rattlesnake abatement to habitat preservation, we create safe environments for both humans and animals.
